Waveshare doesn't publish the color module's thickness in text, only in its 3D drawing files on the product wiki (Resource > Drawing). 6.2 mm is a safe estimate until you measure the module or open that drawing. The breakout boards and their connectors add height. One custom circuit board with a bare OLED panel brings it back down to about 9 mm.
